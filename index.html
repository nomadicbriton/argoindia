About us

Established in 2009, Argo Travel & Recruitment is an MEA-licensed international recruitment consultancy based in Mumbai with strong roots in Kerala. For well over a decade, we have specialised in sourcing, vetting, and deploying high-calibre candidates—primarily from Kerala—to reputable employers across the GCC, Europe, and worldwide markets. We pride ourselves on transparent practices, strict adherence to government regulations, and a proven track record of helping thousands of candidates build successful lives abroad.
